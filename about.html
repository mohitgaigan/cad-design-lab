ABOUT ME

Engineering Ideas Into
Manufacturing-Ready Products

I'm Mohit Kumar, a Product Designer with 5+ years of experience in designing furniture systems, laboratory furniture, sheet metal products and modular workspace solutions. My expertise extends beyond CAD modelling into manufacturing, production support and engineering documentation, allowing me to develop products that are functional, aesthetically refined and ready for fabrication.

Throughout my career I have worked closely with production teams, project managers and clients to transform concepts into practical engineering solutions. From concept development and 3D modelling to manufacturing drawings, product costing and production coordination, I enjoy solving real-world design challenges with a strong focus on Design for Manufacturing (DFM).

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

Engineering Practical, Manufacturing-Ready Solutions

I specialize in developing products that move efficiently from concept to production. My experience combines engineering, manufacturing knowledge and modern CAD tools to deliver practical, cost-effective and production-ready solutions.

Over the years I have contributed to office furniture, laboratory furniture, storage systems, sheet metal products, workstations and customized commercial furniture while working closely with fabrication teams to ensure every design meets manufacturing, quality and cost objectives.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Designing Beyond CAD Models

I believe great product design is achieved when engineering, functionality, aesthetics and manufacturing work together from the beginning of the development process. Every design should solve a real problem while remaining practical to manufacture, cost-effective and durable throughout its lifecycle.

My design approach focuses on understanding user requirements, optimizing manufacturing processes and creating products that balance performance, appearance and production efficiency.

Whether designing laboratory furniture, modular workstations, storage systems or sheet metal products, my objective is to deliver engineering solutions that simplify production while maintaining quality and long-term reliability.
