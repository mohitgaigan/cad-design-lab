ABOUT ME

Designing Products That
Move From Concept To Production

I'm Mohit Kumar, a Product Designer with nearly five years of experience developing furniture systems, laboratory furniture, sheet metal products and modular workspace solutions. My work combines engineering knowledge, practical manufacturing experience and modern CAD tools to create production-ready products that balance functionality, aesthetics and manufacturing efficiency.

Throughout my career I've been involved in every stage of product development—from understanding client requirements and preparing concepts to detailed CAD modelling, realistic visualization, manufacturing documentation and production support. I enjoy solving complex design challenges by transforming ideas into practical products that are efficient to manufacture, reliable in use and visually appealing.

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

Product Designer with a Manufacturing Mindset

My expertise extends beyond CAD modelling. I focus on developing products that can move smoothly from concept to manufacturing while maintaining quality, cost efficiency and functional performance. Over the years I have contributed to corporate office furniture, laboratory furniture, modular workstations, aluminium partition systems and sheet metal products, collaborating closely with production teams to ensure every design is practical and ready for fabrication.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

My Approach to Product Design

I believe successful product design is achieved when engineering, functionality, aesthetics and manufacturing work together as one process—not as separate stages.

Every product should solve a real problem, be comfortable for the user, remain practical to manufacture and provide lasting value throughout its lifecycle.

My objective is to create designs that not only look professional but also simplify production, reduce fabrication complexity and improve the overall user experience.
