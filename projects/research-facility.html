PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designing Functional Spaces for Everyday Use

This project focused on creating a cohesive interior environment for a research facility by designing functional, comfortable and visually balanced spaces that support day-to-day operations.

The scope included reception areas, executive offices, conference facilities and supporting workspaces. Every area was planned with careful consideration for circulation, ergonomics, user interaction and manufacturing feasibility, ensuring that each furniture element could be efficiently produced while maintaining a professional appearance.

RECEPTION AREAS

First Impression Matters

The reception spaces were designed to create a welcoming environment while maintaining a clean, professional and modern identity throughout the facility.

Main Reception Area

The main entrance reception was planned as the primary interaction point for visitors. The design incorporates a reception desk, comfortable waiting area and clear circulation paths while maintaining a contemporary aesthetic suitable for a government research environment.

Lobby Reception

The lobby connects multiple departments and provides an open, visually balanced waiting space. Furniture placement was carefully planned to maximize circulation without compromising comfort.

Wet Laboratory Lobby

Designed as a transition space between laboratory functions, this area combines durable materials with clean visual lines to create an organized and efficient environment for staff and visitors.
