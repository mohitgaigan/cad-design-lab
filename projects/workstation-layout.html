Project Overview

This project involved designing a modular workstation system together with aluminium partition cabins for a corporate office environment. Rather than planning the complete office architecture, the focus was on developing production-ready workstation furniture and cabin solutions that could be efficiently integrated into the client's workspace. The design process covered workstation development, partition planning, ergonomic considerations, furniture integration, realistic visualization, and manufacturing-ready detailing to ensure the final solution could move smoothly from concept to production. Two layout concepts were developed during the design phase to evaluate space utilization, circulation, employee privacy and overall workspace efficiency before selecting the final arrangement.

Client Requirements

The client required a modular workstation solution capable of supporting multiple users while maintaining efficient circulation, executive privacy and a professional corporate appearance. The furniture system also needed to be practical for manufacturing, simple to install and flexible enough to accommodate future workspace expansion without significant redesign.

Layout Development

Two layout concepts were developed to evaluate workstation planning, employee movement, circulation paths and future scalability before finalizing the preferred workspace arrangement.

Concept A – Open Collaborative Workspace

The first concept focused on creating an open working environment that encouraged collaboration while maintaining efficient circulation between workstations. Seating positions were arranged to maximize space utilization without creating congestion.

Concept B – Semi-Private Work Environment

The second concept introduced aluminium partition systems to provide additional privacy while preserving the openness of the office. This layout improved visual organization and created dedicated executive and team working zones.

Modular Workstation Design

The workstation system was designed as a modular solution capable of supporting future expansion while maintaining ergonomics, durability and efficient manufacturing.
