Project Overview

This project focused on designing modular workstation systems for a corporate office environment. The design process included workstation planning, aluminium partition integration, furniture arrangement and realistic 3D visualization. Instead of designing a conventional office layout, the objective was to create efficient workspaces that maximize employee productivity while maintaining privacy, proper circulation and manufacturing feasibility. Two layout concepts were developed to compare workstation arrangements and partition configurations before finalizing the preferred solution.

Layout Development

Two workstation concepts were developed to evaluate circulation, workspace utilization and employee privacy before selecting the final configuration.

Layout Concept 01

Open workstation planning designed for collaborative working with maximum seating capacity and clear circulation paths.

Layout Concept 02

Semi-private workstation configuration using aluminium partition systems to improve employee privacy while maintaining an organized workspace.

Modular Workstation Design

The workstation system was designed to provide a collaborative working environment while maintaining ergonomics, durability and manufacturing efficiency. The modular approach allows future expansion without major structural changes.

6 User Modular Workstation

Designed with a steel frame structure, modular tabletop, integrated pedestal storage and optimized seating arrangement for six users.

Executive Cabin Design

A compact executive workspace developed using aluminium partition profiles to provide privacy while maintaining visual openness and efficient use of office space.

Executive Cabin

Integrated executive desk with storage cabinet, visitor seating and aluminium partition system.
