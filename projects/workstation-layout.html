PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designing a Modern Modular Workplace

This project focused on the design and development of a modular corporate workstation system integrated with aluminium partition cabins for a professional office environment. The objective was to create an efficient workspace that balances employee collaboration, privacy, ergonomics and manufacturing feasibility while maintaining a clean contemporary aesthetic. Rather than designing the complete office architecture, the scope was centred on production-ready furniture systems that could be manufactured, installed and expanded with minimal modifications. Throughout the project, multiple workstation configurations were evaluated before finalizing the most efficient layout based on space utilization, circulation and functional requirements.

CLIENT REQUIREMENTS

Understanding the Challenge

ID Office System required a modular workstation solution capable of supporting multiple users while maintaining efficient circulation, executive privacy and a clean corporate appearance. The furniture also needed to be practical for manufacturing, easy to install and flexible enough to accommodate future office expansion without major redesign.

DESIGN PROCESS

Layout Development

Multiple planning concepts were explored before selecting the final workspace configuration.

Concept 01

The initial concept emphasized an open collaborative environment, allowing teams to communicate easily while ensuring smooth movement between workstations. The arrangement focused on maximizing seating capacity without creating congested circulation paths.

Concept 02

The second concept introduced aluminium partition systems to create semi-private work zones while maintaining visual openness. Executive cabins and dedicated team spaces improved organization, privacy and overall workplace efficiency.
