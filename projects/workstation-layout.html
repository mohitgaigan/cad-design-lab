PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designing a Modern Modular Workplace

This project focused on designing a complete modular office workstation system integrated with an aluminium partition solution for a contemporary corporate workspace. The objective was to maximize available floor space while maintaining employee comfort, executive privacy and an efficient circulation path throughout the office. Instead of concentrating on architectural planning, the project was dedicated to developing production-ready furniture systems that could be manufactured, installed and expanded with minimal modifications. Multiple workstation layouts were evaluated before selecting the final configuration based on usability, flexibility, aesthetics and manufacturing feasibility.

DESIGN PROCESS

Layout Development

Multiple workstation planning concepts were explored before selecting the final layout. Each iteration focused on improving circulation, seating efficiency and the relationship between collaborative workspaces and executive areas.

Concept 01 – Open Workspace Planning

The first concept emphasized an open office environment where collaboration between team members remained the primary focus. Workstations were arranged to maximize seating capacity while maintaining clear circulation paths throughout the workspace.

Concept 02 – Final Approved Layout

The final layout introduced aluminium partition systems to define executive spaces without compromising the openness of the workplace. The arrangement improved organization, employee privacy and overall operational efficiency while remaining practical for manufacturing and installation.
